# Comparative analysis

Draft A best matches the cited dependency order and keeps each contract freeze
auditable. It unlocks FE-8 immediately after ME-1 and gives W2 an exact merged W1
SHA. Its cost is three PR/check/merge cycles.

Draft B reduces GitHub lifecycle latency but couples a publication-only contract
with a new authenticated endpoint. A W1 review defect would delay ME-1 and FE-8,
and the combined diff weakens the explicit contract-freeze handoff.

Draft A is selected. The review and merge overhead is justified by the safety
boundary and the user-requested normal lifecycle.
